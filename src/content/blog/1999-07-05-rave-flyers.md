I finally fixed the scanner. The driver was for Windows 95 and I am on 98, but the compatibility mode worked after the third reboot.

Here are three flyers from this summer. I scanned them at 150 dpi because 300 dpi made the files too big to upload over 56k.

The warehouse on 5th is still doing Friday nights. Bring a friend, bring water, and do not park in the loading zone or the owner will yell at you. He yelled at my cousin for twenty minutes.

I am not posting the full directions on the public web. E-mail me and I will tell you which unmarked door to use.

Please do not copy the flyer art without asking the artists. They are nice people and they deserve credit.

If you are going on Friday, sign the guestbook and maybe I will see you there.
