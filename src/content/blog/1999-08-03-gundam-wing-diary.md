My cousin got me the Wing Zero model kit for my birthday. It took me four evenings and one bottle of model cement.

The instructions were in Japanese. I do not read Japanese. I used the pictures and hoped.

Things that went well:

- the wings look incredible
- the beam rifle fits in the hand without glue
- the decals are easier than the ones on the Tallgeese

Things that did not:

- I painted the chest the wrong shade of red
- one of the shoulder joints is backwards and I cannot fix it without breaking it
- Chogi ate the sticker sheet

I am leaving the shoulder backwards. It gives the kit personality.

Next up is the Heavyarms. I want to try panel lining with a fine-tip marker. If you have tips, e-mail me. If you have spare decals, also e-mail me.
