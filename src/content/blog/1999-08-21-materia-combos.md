Everyone asks what materia I use, so here it is. This is my endgame setup and I will not apologize for it.

**Cloud**

- Knights of the Round + HP Absorb
- Mimic
- W-Summon
- Enemy Skill (for White Wind, obviously)

**Tifa**

- Deathblow + Added Cut
- Counter Attack
- Cure + All

**Yuffie**

- Steal
- Throw
- Morph

The trick is to put Knights of the Round on the character you control, then Mimic with everyone else. It takes a while to set up but the damage is ridiculous.

For the Ruby Weapon I swap in Hades and a lot of HP Plus. Do not try this at level 40. I learned that the hard way and lost two hours of progress.

Chogi sat on the controller during the final battle. It did not help.
